Why ADA Website Compliance Matters for Small Businesses

1 in 4 Americans lives with a disability. If your website isn't accessible, you're locking out millions of potential customers before they even see what you offer.

It's not just about access — it's about risk. ADA-related lawsuits and demand letters against small business websites have surged in recent years, and a single complaint can cost thousands in legal fees, even for a well-meaning business.

The good news: accessible websites aren't just safer, they're better. Clear navigation, readable text, and well-structured pages improve SEO and the experience for every visitor, not just those with disabilities.

ADA compliance isn't a legal checkbox to dread — it's good business and the right thing to do.
