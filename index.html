About

Meet Jennifer Aquino

Jennifer Aquino is the owner of Business Design Innovations and has more than 16 years of experience helping businesses with websites, digital marketing, accessibility, automation, and technology.

She teaches AI in a practical, beginner-friendly way. Her sessions focus on real tasks, clear explanations, and tools people can begin using right away.

Jennifer works with small business owners, women entrepreneurs, nonprofits, community organizations, and professional teams. Her teaching style is warm, patient, and focused on helping people feel confident with technology.

FAQ

Questions People Ask

Everything you need to know before we connect.

Do I need AI experience?

No. Sessions are designed for beginners and people who want practical support.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes. Consultations and training sessions are available through Zoom.

Do you offer in-person workshops?

Yes. In-person opportunities are available in San Clemente, Orange County, and surrounding areas. Travel requests are also considered.

Can you create a custom presentation?

Yes. Speaking and training sessions can be customized for the audience, industry, event, and learning goals.

Do you train nonprofit organizations?

Yes. Training is available for nonprofit staff, board members, volunteers, and community groups.

What AI tools do you teach?

Training may include ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, NotebookLM, Canva AI, and other tools based on the audience's needs.

Can you help my business set up AI systems?

Yes. Consultations can include tool recommendations, prompts, content workflows, marketing support, and automation planning.

Do you provide follow-up resources?

Yes. Depending on the service, participants may receive prompts, worksheets, tool recommendations, or action steps.
